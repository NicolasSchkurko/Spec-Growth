# DECISIONS

**Append-only** decision log (ADR-lite). It records: bug/discrepancy triage, architecture decisions, **decision-request resolutions**, **granted/rejected waivers**, **protected-zone approvals**, and **⚔ conflict reversals**. Format:

## [YYYY-MM-DD] Decision title
- **Context:** what situation forced a decision
- **Decision:** what was chosen and why
- **Consequences:** what it implies / what remains pending
- **Refs:** file:line · specs · changes
